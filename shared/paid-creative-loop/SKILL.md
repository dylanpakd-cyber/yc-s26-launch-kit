# Paid creative loop

Platform-neutral: {AD_PLATFORM} is whatever ad platform the owner runs (for example a social, search or video ad platform). Metric names below are generic; map them to the platform's own labels and say which you used.

## 1. Read the account first (YC Ad Test Desk)
- Pull the last 7 days by ad and ad group (spend, results, cost per result, click-through rate, cost per thousand impressions, hook rate). Use the account's attribution default and say which window you used.
- Label each ad: WORKING (at or under {TARGET_COST_PER_RESULT}), SOFT (1 to 2x target), BROKEN (over 2x, or zero results at high spend).
- Red flag: an ad or campaign that has spent 4x the target cost per result with zero results is a STOP-GATE HIT. Report it at the top of the read with the evidence. Do not wait for more data to report it. Whether to pause it is the owner's call; never write scale, kill or pause as the bot's verdict.
- Find the leak: hook (low thumb-stop), body (low click-through), or landing page (clicks but no results). Name it plainly.

## 2. Research real references (YC Ad Test Desk)
- Pull real ads from public ad libraries the owner can reach. For each reference record the source link, what to borrow, evidence limits (active is not proof of winning), and our version of the message.
- Never invent metrics, quotes, run times, or landing pages.

## 3. Brief (both bots)
- One concept, one distinct reason to buy. Lead with the buyer's pain in their own words (sales calls, reviews, comments), not features.
- Hooks are the 80/20: start from hooks that already won in the account or in references.
- Scarcity and proof must be real and verifiable (a live tier limit, a real count the owner can show). No stale lines.
- Smoke-test hooks as statics first (same product image, only the callout changes), then graduate image to motion to video. Make 4:5 and 9:16.

## 4. Truth and visual QA (both bots)
- Check every product claim against the owner's verified product facts (in YC Launch Ads: the brand truth pack and the facts ledger). Remove or soften anything unverified.
- Look at the actual pixels: text legibility, cropped product, wrong price, banned words, generation artifacts, banding. No real living person's likeness without the owner's typed yes.
- Count reused scenes or base images as one creative, not several. Many ad platforms group near-duplicates, and even where they do not, the test learns one thing, not several.

## 5. Hand off (both bots)
- List each passing creative with name, angle, hook, and destination with a unique ref or UTM tag, for the owner to upload.
- New concepts go into a new test campaign or ad group; winners move into the owner's winners campaign. Reuse a winning post only when its CTA and link already match the new destination; otherwise rebuild the creative.
- Report short: Done, Blocked, Next.

## Stop rules
No spend, publish, budget, or campaign edits by the bot. The owner makes every live change. Paid generation needs the owner's yes and a credit ceiling. Ask the owner to log every live change they make (ids, before and after).
