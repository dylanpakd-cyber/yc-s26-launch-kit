# Quote lineup scout

Build or refresh a 30-to-60-person quote lineup for a launch post on X. Score each person by how much their followers overlap the product's buyers, not by follower count. Drafts only: the owner picks the lineup and does every outbound action.

## Steps
1. **Start from what was already used.** Ask the owner to paste earlier quote or amplification packs for this launch. Write a used-creators log with every account already used, swapped out, or always skipped. Never put a logged account back in the lineup unless the owner asks for that.
2. **Collect overlap evidence. Reuse what exists first.**
   - Look for audience reports the owner already has (for example an audience-research tool's "who this audience follows" list). Do not create a new paid or quota-costing report unless the owner asks for one.
   - Read the owner's own followers, read-only, to find warm adjacency.
   - Use public keyword search only as a last resort. Mark anyone found that way as "overlap unproven."
3. **Check each candidate's last ten posts.**
   - Leave out the pinned post. Note the date span, the cadence, how many of the ten are on topic, and the median likes.
   - Prefer small, active accounts over large ones that have gone quiet. Send stale accounts (newest post more than about 30 days old) to skip/watch with the evidence.
4. **Score from evidence only (0 to 100).** Add up:
   - audience affinity
   - warm adjacency, meaning they follow the owner
   - freshness
   - topic fit in the last ten posts
   - a small-account bonus
   Subtract points when engagement on the last ten is near zero. Follower count never adds reach points. Never invent engagement or audience quality. If the evidence is missing, say so.
5. **Write up each person.** Give:
   - account
   - audience in one line
   - last-ten note
   - why they fit
   - a suggested unique angle (no two the same)
   - any skip reason
   Split the list into core and bench. Keep a separate skip/watch list that includes brand-safety flags.
6. **Respect the copy locks.** Angles follow the owner's product naming, CTA and banned-words rules ({BANNED_WORDS_FILE}, {CLAIM_LOCKS}). They carry no prices, invented stats, testimonials or deadlines. Mark anything that needs proof with [NEED]. Apply the banned-words list by hand and say "checked by hand".
7. **Deliver a draft.** A sortable table in chat plus a short method note: the scoring rubric, the gaps, and open questions for the owner. Never DM, book, pay, post, quote, reply, like or repost.

## Fallback
Fewer than 10 accounts pass step 3, or the owner has no export: stop and run the NO-NETWORK path in yc-x-launch-desk-network-and-lineup. No network is required to launch.
