# YC X Launch Desk: references

Templates, examples and reference tables for the YC X Launch Desk steps. Everything here is invented and labelled as an example, or is a method, not a result. Copy the shape, never the facts. Never present an example number as the owner's. The checks named here are written checklists in yc-x-launch-desk-checks, applied by hand.

## Worked example: a fictional 48-hour launch

Everything here is invented. No real person, account, price or result. It shows what each step produces and when. The examples pass the hand checks.

The product: a browser add-on that turns a recipe page into a shopping list. T0 is {LAUNCH_DATE} 09:30 {LAUNCH_TZ}.

### T-48h: brief, angle, people plan
- The owner names the KPI (installs from the store dashboard) and a target (150 by T+24h). See the brief example in yc-x-launch-desk-references. The fields check (brief) passes.
- Market card: buyers hate scrolling past life stories and ads to find ingredients. Enemy: recipe pages built for ad views. Argument already running: "recipe sites are unusable". Market card PASS.
- Angle sheet picks "category stance": recipe sites were built for ads, not for cooking. The demonstration angle is kept for the second beat.
- Path: LINEUP. The owner exports followers. 40 accounts get a two-minute check; 31 pass. See the lineup-30 example in yc-x-launch-desk-references for a full 34-row log (30 in the lineup, the lineup-log check PASS) and the lineup example in yc-x-launch-desk-references for a short one.

### T-48h to T-36h: post and video
- Ten first lines. The chosen one: "Recipe sites were built for ads. Not for cooking." the hook check passes (the hooks example in yc-x-launch-desk-references).
- The post (the post example in yc-x-launch-desk-references) passes the facts-ledger check against the ledger example in yc-x-launch-desk-references and the channel check (x) (link placement: reply). Harsh read 6 of 6.
- Video: 50 seconds. Open on the demo itself: a long page collapses into 9 items. No logo. Script card filled. Mute test passes.

### T-36h: quote drafts
- One distinct draft per core and bench account, in each person's voice. Two people prefer to write their own. Status "creator wrote own". The owner reviews.

### T-24h: Test of 300
- The owner fills every slot in yc-x-launch-desk-test-of-300. The run scores the four signals 6, 8, 7, 7. Scroll stop is under 7. Round one rewrite moves the demo to second zero. Round two scores 8, 9, 7, 8. Freeze (the freeze example in yc-x-launch-desk-references).

### T-12h: eight gates
- See the gates example in yc-x-launch-desk-references. Two quote drafts are still in review; the owner accepts that risk in writing. The gates check (check_gates) prints GO WITH RISKS and passes.

### T-1h
- The owner opens the store page on a phone. The quiet window starts.

### T0 to +60 minutes
- Run sheet in the run-sheet example in yc-x-launch-desk-references. At +15 several replies ask "does it work on site X". The owner posts the clarifier. At +60 the contrast line is being quoted, so the owner posts the 15-second clip as the second beat.

### T+1d
- Reply drafts for questions. One refund-style complaint is escalated to the owner with no draft. Thank-you drafts for the people who quoted, each different.

### T+3d: retro
- See the retro example in yc-x-launch-desk-references. Verdict PARTIAL. The first broken link was the destination: the page did not say which sites work. The top backlog angle is "which sites it works on", asked by the most people.
