WHEN TO USE: the owner asks "we're live, what now?", replies, the first hour, the second beat; this skill covers the live window run sheet and the day after. WHEN NOT: the post-launch review (retro-and-angles). One-off asks can load this skill directly; a full run reaches it through yc-x-launch-desk-workflow.
# SKILL: wave-and-replies

Purpose: guide the owner through the live window and the day after, using what the post is doing, without automating anything. The owner reads the numbers and tells the desk. The desk drafts. The owner acts.

## Use when
- Before T0, to prepare the run sheet. At T0, when the owner says "it's live".
- The owner says "run sheet", "wave", "second beat", "what do I do now", "draft replies", "reply to this", "who reposted", "stay online plan".

## Inputs
- T0, the frozen post, the link placement and the brief.
- Reply owner and backup, escalation owner. Default: the founder for all three, with a note that a backup is safer.
- The unused first lines, the 15-second cut and the angle sheet (raw material for the second beat).
- During the wave: what the owner reports (comments, quotes, link clicks, KPI count so far). The desk does not read or act on the account.

## Steps: Part A, the run sheet (prepared before T0)
1. Fill the run-sheet template in yc-x-launch-desk-references ("Templates") and run the fields check (run-sheet) before T0.
2. **T0 to +5 minutes:** the owner confirms the post is live and the link works on a phone. Reply to the first genuine comments in the owner's voice from prepared drafts. No self-promotional replies. Do not delete or edit unless something is wrong.
3. **+15 minutes:** read the early signal the owner reports. Are the first comments about the idea or about confusion? If confusion, post the prepared one-line clarifier reply (the owner posts it).
4. **+60 minutes:** decide on the second beat. What did people quote? If one line keeps being repeated, build on it.
5. Stay in the replies for the planned window. The author replying keeps the conversation alive.

## Steps: Part B, the second beat (only if something true and new exists)
1. Candidates, drafted before T0: a short clip of the strongest moment; an honest update ("what we learned in the first hour", only with real numbers the owner reports); a reply with the demo; a clarification of the most common misunderstanding.
2. Pick at +60 minutes only if it is true and new. Otherwise do nothing. Doing nothing is a valid choice.
3. Never invent a cap, deadline or number to force urgency. Never post the same thing twice.

## Steps: Part C, replies
1. Sort what the owner pastes: questions, objections, praise, trolls, support, press, people who quoted.
2. Draft answers in the owner's voice: short, specific, honest. Answer objections with a fact from the ledger or a plain "fair point".
3. Do not argue with trolls. Suggest the owner ignore or mute.
4. Escalate to the owner, no draft: refunds, legal, complaints, press, anything about a named person.
5. Run the style check and the facts-ledger check on reply drafts that hold numbers.

## Steps: Part D, repost list and stay-online plan
1. List people who quoted or reposted with commentary, from what the owner pastes.
2. Draft a thank-you for the owner to send by hand, if they want. Each one different.
3. Note whose audiences responded, for the retro.
4. Plan the owner's next day: when to check in, when to rest, when the run sheet ends.

## Gate
- The fields check (run-sheet) passes before T0: T0, the +5, +15 and +60 plans, the second beat, the clarifier reply, replies owner, escalation owner, stay-online end time.
- Every reply and second beat is true. Every number is in the ledger.
- The owner does every outbound action.

## Output
```
RUN SHEET: <filled template>
+5: ... | +15: ... | +60: ...
SECOND BEAT: ready: <clip | update | demo reply | clarifier> | used: yes/no, why
REPLY DRAFTS: 1. <to whom, paraphrased> -> <draft>
ESCALATIONS: ...
REPOST LIST: ...
STAY-ONLINE PLAN: ...
```

## If things go wrong
- **The link breaks.** Run the rollback plan from gate G8: the owner posts a reply with the working link, and the page owner fixes the page. Draft the reply now.
- **Early comments are confused.** Post the clarifier. Do not edit the post unless the confusion comes from a factual error.
- **Nothing happens by +60.** Do not panic-post. Check that the post is visible to others and that the link works. Then follow the plan. Write it down for the retro.
- **A hostile pile-on.** Stop drafting replies to it. Escalate to the owner. Suggest muting.
- **Round cap.** Two drafts per reply. Then the owner writes their own.

## Never
- Never post, reply, like, repost, quote, DM or schedule.
- Never read or act on the owner's account directly.
- Never invent a number, a cap or a deadline.
- Never draft a reply to a legal, refund or press message.

Revision cap: two revision rounds per piece of work. After round 2, stop, show the best version with its open flags, and ask the owner how to go on.
Hand checks: this bot runs no scripts. Every check named in this skill is a written checklist in yc-x-launch-desk-checks. Apply it by hand, line by line, and write "checked by hand" next to the result.
