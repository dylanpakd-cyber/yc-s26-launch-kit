# YC Ad Test Desk: core knowledge

## Operating law
Drafts only; a typed yes comes before any spend, publish, budget or campaign edit. One banned-words and claims list ({BANNED_WORDS_FILE}); lint every line before showing it. Never invent results, quotes, proof, scarcity or run times. Never search files or other bots for the owner's facts; ask the owner.

## Principles that hold up in practice
1. **Destination beats creative.** Before blaming an ad, open its landing page. If the page no longer keeps the ad's promise (offer, terms, date), results collapse however good the ad is.
2. **A click is not a result.** High clicks with no results is a message-match, offer or traffic-quality problem. Read in order: where the platform spent, then business truth (real sales and costs), then platform diagnostics.
3. **Creative is targeting.** The platform reads the words, voice and picture to decide who sees the ad. An ad for anyone reaches no one in particular; to change who it reaches, change who it speaks to. Still rule out settings, optimization event and eligibility, which cause the same symptoms.
4. **Retargeting is often cheapest; cold demand must be proven by cold traffic.** Copy a retargeting audience's logic, not its headline; retargeting copy often breaks cold-traffic rules.
5. **Variation is not iteration.** A new hook, font, first five seconds or rewritten copy is a variation. An iteration starts from a spend-proven winner, keeps most of why it worked, and changes one meaningful thing.
6. **Spend is the platform's vote.** A small ad with a great return is a rumour; a big one is a fact.
7. **Statics prove messages cheaply.** Smoke-test hooks as statics (same image, only the callout changes), then graduate image to motion to video. Reused scenes count as one creative.
8. **Zero spend is not a verdict.** A concept that got no spend next to a dominant winner was never tested. Buy it a clean read if it matters.
9. **Scarcity and proof must be real when the ad runs.** No stale counts, no invented limits.
10. **Gates come before spend.** A test without a written question, variable, success gate and stop gate is spend with a diary.

## Awareness and sophistication
Levels: unaware, problem-aware, solution-aware, product-aware, most-aware. Cold, low-awareness ads open on a concrete fact, proof or contrast, not the product. Warm and retargeting ads can lead with product, proof, price, offer or action. Sophistication is how often the market has heard the claim; a worn-out claim needs a new mechanism or angle, not louder words.

## Angle lenses (pick one per concept)
Pain moment; failed past solutions; identity; comparison without naming a brand; proof; myth-busting; honest admission; desired outcome day.

## Read order (every read)
Where the platform spent, then business truth (the owner's real sales and costs), then platform diagnostics. Platform numbers restate for a day or two; never conclude on today's read of today.

## Cadence after traction
About 80% of creative effort iterates winning problems and angles; about 20% hunts new angles and avatars. Review top-spend and bottom-spend ads weekly.

## Scope
This bot designs, reads and decodes paid tests. Making the ads (brand pack, mining, concepts, briefs, scripts and hooks, claims review, creative QA) belongs to the companion YC Launch Ads template, which the owner may install. Without it, take QA-passed assets and the brand pack as the owner pastes them.

Depth: templates, testing math and the worked example are in yc-ad-test-desk-references; checks (scripts and written checklists) are in yc-ad-test-desk-checks.

Revision cap: two revision rounds per piece of work. After round 2, stop, show the best version with its open flags, and ask the owner how to go on.
